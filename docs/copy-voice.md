# How SigmaFlow sounds

Gokul's pick, 19 Oct 2026: SigmaFlow's words are written the way
[tryvoiceink.com](https://tryvoiceink.com/) is written. This page says what that means,
so every new screen, button and line sounds like the same product. The first open
([docs/mockups/onboarding.html](mockups/onboarding.html), round 6) is the worked example.

## The rules

1. **Say what the person gets, not what the app does inside.** VoiceInk's headline is
   "Write at the speed of thought", not "Local speech recognition". Ours: "You talk faster
   than you type." Never name a model, a file format or a mechanism.
2. **Two short sentences: you do this, SigmaFlow does that.** "Speak naturally.
   VoiceInk writes and formats it for you." Ours: "Speak naturally. SigmaFlow writes it
   for you." The person acts in the first sentence; SigmaFlow does the work in the second.
3. **SigmaFlow is the one doing the work, by name.** "SigmaFlow writes it", "SigmaFlow
   keeps working from up here". Not "it is transcribed", not "the text is inserted".
4. **Plain, everyday nouns.** "Mail, Slack, Notes, anywhere you type", "a desk Mac and a
   MacBook". Never "application", "input field", "transcription engine".
5. **Show it, with real-sounding people.** VoiceInk proves cleanup with "What you say /
   What you get" and names like Maya and Sarah. When a feature changes words, show the
   before and after instead of describing the change.
6. **Feature names are what it does for you.** "Writes clearly and concisely", "Writes
   for the inbox", "Adds quick notes to your journal". A verb, then the result.
7. **Short, confident, warm.** "It's yours." "Hands free, whenever you need it." No
   hedging ("may", "should", "try to"), no exclamation marks, no jokes.
8. **Privacy is a promise, said once, where it matters.** "Your recordings stay yours."
   Say it on the screen where the person makes that choice (permissions, the folder),
   not on every screen.
9. **Small badges are three to four words.** "Runs on your Mac", "Words as you speak",
   "Works in every app".

## What stays the same

- **How much text a screen carries is still screen by screen.** Onboarding has a line or
  two under each title (Gokul, 19 Oct 2026). The app's own pages stay lean: titles and
  buttons, and a line only when the person can act on it. When a line exists, it follows
  the rules above.
- No em dashes and no middle dots, anywhere.
- Contractions are fine ("you're", "it's"), written with a curly apostrophe.
