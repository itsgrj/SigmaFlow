Transcript graph

Round 2, 18 Oct 2026. The graph is now the third tab on Transcriptions, beside History and Insights, and it reads your meetings and conversations as well as your dictations. Every name and topic you say becomes a dot; two dots are joined when they were said together, in one dictation or one line of a meeting, and the more often, the stronger the line. Hover a dot to light up what it is joined to, drag dots around, scroll to zoom, drag the empty space to move. The meetings, dates and counts follow a real history; every topic and name is a made-up stand-in, and the sentences in the panel are made up too.

What counts as a topic

Names, products and short forms: anything said with a capital letter in the middle of a sentence, plus every word in your Dictionary. Ordinary words like So, Maybe or Monday are left out, and a topic needs at least 4 mentions to get a dot.

Mishearings show up as their own dots (Mira and Myra, Moring and Mooring), which is a quick way to spot a word the Dictionary should fix.

Clicking

Clicking a dot opens a panel beside the graph with every line it was said in, newest first: dictations with their time, meeting lines with the meeting and the moment, which open that meeting's transcript there. It settles in a second or two and then stays still.
