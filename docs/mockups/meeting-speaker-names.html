Speaker names

Give a speaker a real name, and every line from that voice in the meeting takes it. SigmaFlow also keeps the voice under that name, without saying so anywhere, so the next meeting the same person speaks in starts with their name already on their lines. Try it: click a speaker name in screen 01, type, press Return, then open screen 02. Lines are from your 14 Oct meeting as the new build hears it.

A name applies to the whole meeting

Renaming Speaker 2 renames every Speaker 2 line, and the .md changes with it. Empty the field and press Return to go back to the number. Esc leaves it as it was.

Typing the name into the .md by hand also counts, the app reads it back

Why choice 03 matters

One person sometimes comes out as two speakers. Giving both the same name is the quickest repair, and A treats it as that: one person, one voice kept, Speakers drops by one.

Nothing in the app says it remembered

The names are simply there. No tag, no hint, no setting. A voice it is not sure about stays Speaker 1, 2: a missing name is better than the wrong one.

Renaming again teaches it again

Correct a name in a later meeting and that is the name from then on. Transcribe again keeps the names too, because it hears the same voices.
