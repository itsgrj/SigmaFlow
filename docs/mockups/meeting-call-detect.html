Recording calls

Two additions to Meetings. One: when Zoom, Teams, FaceTime or a browser turns your microphone on, SigmaFlow asks if you want to record the call. Two: a call is recorded from both sides, your microphone and the sound of the call itself, so the others are heard even with AirPods in, and the transcript labels your lines as yours. Four choices are open. Each switch redraws every screen.

First time only

macOS asks once to let SigmaFlow hear the call. "First time: the macOS prompt" shows it.

The same row as today

The overlay's dot, REC, the timer and one row of green dots, with Stop beside it. The dots move whenever anyone on the call talks.

Your lines are always yours

Everything from your microphone is labelled as you, with nothing to guess. Only the call side is split into Speaker 1, Speaker 2, by how the voices sound.

Your label is drawn in brighter ink than the others, so your own lines are easy to find

What can still go wrong

Two similar voices on the call can end up under one speaker label. In the test, two of three computer voices did. That can only happen among the other people, never with you.
