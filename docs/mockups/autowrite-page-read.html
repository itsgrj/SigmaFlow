What Autowrite read, round 2

Asked 12 Nov 2026: the page card that replaced the screenshot (browser-extension 09) is out. The screenshot stays in every design. The new piece has one job: show that SigmaFlow read more than the screen, the whole page, scrolled-away parts too. These are eight ways, each drawn on its own, at the overlay's real proportions, made 1.5 times larger to read here. Where the extension can't read (another app, a site switched off), the overlay is today's: the screenshot alone.

Reply with the number you want. Made-up ticket, as before.

For comparison

Before the extension: the screenshot alone

A. The picture tells it, no words

01A tag on the picture

The screenshot as today, with WHOLE PAGE in a small black tag across its corner. The least change: the picture stays the picture, and two words say the rest.

02The whole page as a strip, the screen framed in itMy pick

The picture becomes a tall, narrow copy of the whole page. The part that was on your screen is bright, in a white frame. What's above and below it is shaded: read, but not on screen. It shows "more than the screen" without a word. It's as tall as the two lines of words, so the overlay grows no taller.

03A stack

The screenshot sits on two sheets offset behind it, like more pages under it, with the number of words read under your words. Familiar from photo apps: one shown, more behind.

04The picture runs through the page once

The moment the page is read, the screenshot runs down the whole page in about two seconds, then settles back on what was on your screen. It moves once per Autowrite (the loop here is only for looking). The only option with motion. SigmaFlow's rule is that nothing blinks, and this doesn't, but it is new.

B. The picture plus a few words

05A caption under the picture

A slightly larger screenshot with the site and the word count under it, like a photo's caption. Everything about what was read sits in one column, apart from what you said.

06A line under your words

The screenshot as today. Under what you said, one faint line: "Read the whole page, 1,240 words". It reads as a sentence, in the spot the eye goes next.

07In the status row

The screenshot as today. WHOLE PAGE sits at the right end of the row with AUTOWRITE and the timer, faint, like a mode. Nothing moves in the words' area.

08A bigger picture, the page's title across its foot

The screenshot grows to 150 points wide, with a black band across its foot holding the page's title and the word count. The picture leads, and the overlay is about two lines taller.
