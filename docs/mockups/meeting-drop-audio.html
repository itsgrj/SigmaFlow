Drop a recording

Drag any recording from Finder onto the Meetings page and it becomes a meeting like the ones you record: its own folder in SigmaFlow/Meetings, transcribed on this Mac the moment it lands. Your file stays where it was; SigmaFlow keeps its own copy. Try it: drag a real audio file from Finder onto the window below, or use the buttons above the window.

01 While you drag over the page

02 The meeting's date

03 The name

04 Saying it can be done

It lands where its date puts it

With 02 A the sample file, recorded on 26 September, goes into the September group further down the list, not at the top. With 02 B it goes to the top, dated now.

The time and length in the name come from the file either way

Any recording the Mac can play

Voice Memos, Zoom and Meet downloads (video files too, only the sound is kept), MP3, WAV and the rest. Several at once is fine, they are transcribed one after another.

WhatsApp voice notes (.ogg, .opus) can't be opened, the last state shows that line
