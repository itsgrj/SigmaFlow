The 31 books

Picked so anyone would know them, and spread from short to long, so the sentence always names a book: The Cat in the Hat on the first day, short classics in the first weeks, the long ones (War and Peace, all of Harry Potter, In Search of Lost Time at 1.27 million words) only after years.

Word counts are the commonly published figures for the English editions.

Black and white

All 31 covers are grey, like everything else in the app. The ones your words are longer than are bright; the longer ones stay dark. Nothing marks a book as done or next, and nothing happens when you pass one: it is a fun fact about how much you have said.

The covers

Real covers from Open Library, one English edition each. This page loads them from the internet; in the app they ship inside SigmaFlow, so the shelf works offline. They belong to their publishers: fine on your own Mac, a question to settle before SigmaFlow is ever sold or handed out.
