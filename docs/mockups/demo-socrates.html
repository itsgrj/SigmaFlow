Styles

Three, bound to ⌥1 to ⌥3. Each shows what it does to the same plain sentence.

Styles3 styles
ST1
⌥1 Socratic
Turns any statement into a question.
“We ship on Friday.” becomes “Do we ship on Friday, and what do we mean by ship?”
ST2
⌥2 Plato's draft
Tidy, formal prose, ready for the record.
“We ship on Friday.” becomes “It was agreed that the work would be delivered on Friday.”
ST3
⌥3 The oracle
Short, and slightly cryptic.
“We ship on Friday.” becomes “Friday. If the gods allow.”
