SigmaFlow, a look at every page

I went through every page and tab of the installed 1.0.0 build, drawn by the app itself on 16 Oct 2026, and compared each one with how Otter, Grain, Fireflies, Wispr Flow, Loom, Zendesk and others handle the same thing on Mobbin. Nothing in the app has changed. Each finding has a Before, redrawn from the real page, and an After. Dashed outlines mark what changes. Where an After says it is interactive, you can click or point at it.

Mark Do it or Skip on each finding, then press Copy picks and paste the result to me. I skipped the Custom styles tab because it reads the Keychain when it opens, so the app could not draw it without a password prompt.

01

Words you read get a reading face

Geist Pixel is SigmaFlow's voice, and it should stay on titles, the sidebar, labels and buttons. But you also read whole paragraphs in it: 3,328 dictations, every meeting line, every snippet. Pixel letters are built from squares, so long text tires the eye. Every reading-heavy app on Mobbin uses one plain face for the words themselves. Plain Geist already ships inside SigmaFlow, so this swaps fonts the app already has. It touches the transcript lines, dictation text and snippet text, nothing else.

03

Row buttons only on the row you point at

Transcriptions shows Copy and Delete on every row, and Dictionary and Snippets show Edit and Delete on every row. That puts 8 to 10 buttons on screen that you aren't using, and Delete is the easiest thing to hit, which goes against your rule that delete is hard to find. ElevenLabs and Zendesk show actions only on the row under the pointer, and put delete inside a ⋯ menu. The same change applies to all three pages.

An Add button instead of an open form

Dictionary and Snippets open on an empty form with a greyed-out Add button and a Cancel that has nothing to cancel. It is the first thing you see, and you use it rarely. Zendesk's Shortcuts page and Grammarly's dictionary put one Add button beside search, and the form opens only when you ask for it. Snippets gets search too, so the two pages match.

Styles: the third tile's name, shorter tiles

The third default style is named "Retarded". That word is a slur for people with intellectual disabilities, and it is what anyone you show SigmaFlow to will remember. I'd rename it for what it does: Lowercase is my pick, with Texting or Relaxed as other options. The tiles are also about twice as tall as their content, so trimming them makes the page readable at a glance. The line under the tiles goes too (see 02).

A Get ready checklist for a new Mac

SigmaFlow needs the microphone and Accessibility, and without Accessibility every shortcut stops without telling you. Today a new user gets the system prompts at launch with no context, a Grant item in the menu bar and a card deep in Settings. Loom keeps a small checklist in the corner until setup is done, and Wispr Flow finishes setup with a practice dictation. This one has four steps and disappears once all are ticked. It matters most if SigmaFlow ever goes to someone else's Mac.

14

How much each speaker talked

The speaker row under a meeting's title only shows names. Grain and Fireflies show each speaker's share of the talking. It makes a wrong split easy to spot too: if a room of five reads as two voices at 50% each, you can see it at a glance, which ties in with the speaker problem you raised on 14 Oct. It's small, but it's a new feature, so it should be its own ask.
