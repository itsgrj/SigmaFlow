    A. The picture shows there was more

    01The fold lineMy pick

    The picture becomes the whole page, tall and narrow, with a dashed line where your screen ended, as Hotjar marks the fold. Above the line is what you saw; below it is what SigmaFlow read as well. One look says "it read past my screen". Mobbin: Hotjar.

    02Your screen framed in the whole page

    The same tall picture, but your screen's part sits in a white frame and the rest is shaded. Says the same as 01, with a frame instead of a line. Mobbin: Evernote's page rail.

    03A page rail

    The screenshot, and beside it a narrow column of the page's other screens, small, the first outlined: the one you were looking at. Reads like a document with pages. Mobbin: Zillow, Dovetail.

    04A filmstrip under the picture

    The same idea laid flat: the screenshot on top, a strip of the page's screens under it, yours outlined. Wider than tall, so it suits a long page better than 03.

    05The picture runs down the page once

    Once the page is read, the screenshot scrolls the whole page in about two seconds, then rests back on your screen. Once per Autowrite; the loop here is only for looking. The only option with motion.

    06A scrollbar on the picture

    The screenshot as today, with a thin scrollbar along its right edge. The handle's size shows your screen was a third of the page. Quiet; it shows the page is longer more than that it was read, so it pairs well with 16.

    07A stack with a count

    The screenshot on top of sheets, and a small 4 in its corner: four screens' worth were read. Familiar from photo apps that stack a burst.

    B. The picture and what was found in it

    08The words on the picture, marked as read

    The screenshot's lines of text are drawn dark, like text that's been read or selected, so it reads "it read this" rather than "it looked at this". The lines run on past the picture's bottom edge. Mobbin: Matter, Pocket.

    09Scan corners

    Four white corners around the screenshot, as a document scanner frames a page, with WHOLE PAGE under it. It reads as "scanned", which is closer to the truth than "photographed". Mobbin: ABY Journal, Craft.

    10Dots on what it used

    A slightly larger screenshot with dots pinned on the messages it's answering, and the name of the one it'll reply to. The most specific option: it shows not only that it read, but what it will answer. It needs the extension to say which message, which it can. Mobbin: Shop, Etsy.

    C. Like an attachment

    11Two attachments above your words

    As an AI chat box shows what's attached: the screenshot and the page, side by side over what you said, which then runs the full width below. It's the layout people already know from ChatGPT. Mobbin: ChatGPT, Mistral.

    12A link preview

    The screenshot becomes the picture of a link preview card, with the page's title and site under it, the way a link unfurls in WhatsApp or iMessage. Mobbin: WhatsApp.

    13A file card

    A card with a header (site and title) and the screenshot under it, as Slack previews a shared file. The header says what it is; the picture shows it. Mobbin: Slack.

    14"Based on" under your words

    The screenshot moves under your words, into one line: "From" with the screenshot inline, then the site. Reads like a source note under an answer. Mobbin: ManyChat.

    15A sources pill

    The screenshot as today, and under your words a pill with the screenshot and a page, overlapping, then "Screen and page". ChatGPT gathers its sources the same way. Mobbin: ChatGPT.

    D. Saying what it's doing

    16A line that says what it's reading

    The screenshot as today; under your words, a line that changes: "Reading help.kettle.com" while you hold the key, then "Read the whole page, 1,240 words". Two states drawn. Mobbin: Dropbox Dash.

    17A count on the picture

    The screenshot as today with 1/4 in its corner, as a gallery shows the first of four photos: your screen was one of four screens read.

    18Screenshot plus page

    Two things, joined by a plus: the screenshot, and a small page with the word count. The plainest picture of "this, and also all of this".

    19A title bar over the overlay

    The screenshot as today; across the top of the overlay, like a window's title bar, the site and the page's title, faint, with WHOLE PAGE at its end. Everything about the page sits in one line above, and the overlay stays tidy.

    20Hover the picture to see the whole page

    The screenshot as today. Put the pointer on it and the whole page opens above, tall, your screen framed in it. Nothing extra shows unless you look. The overlay doesn't take the pointer today, so this needs that changed. Mobbin: Pinterest.
