Fix spelling as you type, in the browser

Picks, 16 Nov 2026: 02 04 06 08. Built the same day.

Asked 16 Nov 2026: now that SigmaFlow has a browser extension, fix a misspelled word the moment you move on to the next one. macOS already does this in Mail, Notes and Messages. Chrome, Dia, Arc and the other Chromium browsers don't, and until the extension SigmaFlow couldn't see their text boxes.

Most of it was settled in the chat (below). What is left is three small places it can be seen, with 8 options. Reply with the numbers you want, one from each group, for example "02 04 08". Option 06 combines with 04 or 05. Names and sites are made up, and the browser is drawn as Dia.

    Already decided, 16 Nov 2026

    • On by default, with a switch to turn it off. Where the switch sits is group B.
    • One word at a time. When you type a space or punctuation after a word, the extension sends that word to SigmaFlow on this Mac. The Mac's own spell checker (the one behind macOS's "Correct spelling automatically") decides, with the words you've taught your Mac, and your SigmaFlow dictionary on top. A fix comes back in a few milliseconds and is put in as if typed, so ⌘Z undoes it.
    • Delete right after takes it back, and that word is never changed again. Where it is remembered is group C.
    • Never touched: words in your dictionary, words in capitals, words with digits, web addresses and email addresses, text inside code, password boxes, and words not written in Latin letters (Tamil is left alone). Sites switched off in the toolbar card are left alone too.
    • If you've moved on, it gives up. When the word before the cursor is no longer the one that was checked (you kept typing into it, clicked elsewhere), the fix is dropped.
    • Works in text boxes and the editors most web apps use: Gmail, Cliq on the web, Slack, Notion, Linear, help desks. Not in Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, Figma, or code editors. Electron apps, Safari and Firefox stay out of reach.
    • Before the Chrome Web Store. The submission is still paused, so this ships in the extension's first store version, not an update.

    The new privacy words

    PRIVACY.md changes in the same commit. "Two moments" becomes three, and this goes first:

    PRIVACY.MD, THE BROWSER EXTENSION
    The extension helps the SigmaFlow app on your Mac in three moments, and does nothing at any other time.
    • While you type in a text box on a website. Each time you finish a word, it sends that one word to SigmaFlow on your Mac, which checks the spelling and sends back a fix if there is one. Nothing you type is kept, and nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn this off in SigmaFlow, or for one site from the toolbar button. It never reads password boxes.
    THE EXTENSION'S DESCRIPTION (MANIFEST AND STORE)
    Fixes typos as you type, learns from your fixes on websites, and lets Autowrite read the whole conversation. Works with SigmaFlow on your Mac.

    One fix, as it happens

    For reference, the same for every option below: you type a typo, then a space; the word is fixed before your next letter. Press Delete straight after and your word comes back, with the space.

    A. What a fix looks like

    01Nothing shows, the word just changes

    Like iPhone after you've stopped looking. Quiet, and nothing is drawn into anyone's page. The cost: a wrong fix in a long message can go unnoticed until it's sent.

    02A faint dotted line under the fixed word, for 2 seconds

    Like the line macOS draws under a word it corrected. Enough to catch your eye if you're looking, gone before it's in the way. Drawn by the extension over the page, so the text itself is never changed by it.

    03Your word, small, above the fix, for 2 seconds

    Like the bubble macOS shows while it corrects. You see what it was and what it became, and a click on the bubble puts your word back, as Delete does. The most to see, and the only one you can take back with the pointer.

    B. Where the switch sits

    04Settings, Connections, under Your browser

    Beside the browsers it works in. One switch for every browser. Its caption says how to take a fix back, the one thing nobody would guess.

    05The Dictionary's footer, under Learn from my corrections

    Next to the other switch about your words. The fixes come from your dictionary and the Mac's, so it lives with them. Further from the browsers it works in.

    06Also in the extension's toolbar card

    The card already has a switch for the site you're on (which stops fixes there too). This adds one row that turns fixes off in that browser, everywhere. It's the same setting as 04 or 05, so turning it off in one place turns it off in the other. Combines with 04 or 05.

    C. Where a word you kept goes

    07Remembered quietly, out of sight

    SigmaFlow keeps a list on this Mac of words you took back, and never changes them again. Nothing to see, and nothing to manage. The cost: no way to undo a keep, short of typing the word wrong on purpose and fixing it by hand.

    08Into your Dictionary, tagged LEARNED

    The kept word becomes a word in your Dictionary, like a fix SigmaFlow learned from you, so you can see it and delete it. It also helps dictation: a name you type the way you want (Brewly, not Brewery) is then heard that way when you say it. Delete all learned clears these too.
