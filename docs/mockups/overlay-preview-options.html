Overlay, tried with your own voice

Option A from the last round, now with two changes. Every preview ends with the words being pasted into a text box, so Minimal visibly hands over the same words as Live transcript, just without showing them while you talk. And you can try it: hold the key, speak, and the previews use what you actually said instead of a sample line.

Mockup only. Press and hold Hold to talk with the cursor and speak. Your push-to-talk key is left out on purpose: it would also bring up the real overlay, and you would be watching three at once. Chrome will ask for the microphone the first time, and its own speech recognition stands in for SigmaFlow's. If the mic is blocked, the previews play a sample line while you hold.
