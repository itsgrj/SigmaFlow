Your own notes, round 1

Dropped, 19 Oct 2026, nothing picked. Taking notes pulls people out of the conversation they are having. SigmaFlow records with one key and asks nothing more of them during it.

The idea from Granola's notepad: while a meeting or a conversation records, you can write a few words of your own, like "Ravi owns the launch email". Each note is stamped with the moment it was written and saved in the meeting's .md beside what was being said then. Three things to pick.

01How you write a note

Each card plays the same moment: someone says Ravi will do the launch email, and you note it down. The grey keys at the bottom left show what is being pressed; they are not part of the app.

AA line at the foot of the notes card

Click it, type, Return. While you type, the card takes the keyboard; Return or Esc gives it back to the app you were in. Conversations only: a meeting recording has no card.

BRight ⌘ + N opens a note bar

Over whatever app is in front, in a meeting or a conversation. Type, Return, it ticks and closes. It sits with Right ⌘ + S, the screenshot key, and has the same brief HUD flash.

CHold Right ⌘ + N and say it

Dictation, but the words go into the notes instead of being typed. The room hears you, and those seconds are left out of the recording, as dictation already is.

02Where a note sits in the transcript

The meeting after Stop, with two notes written during it. Under each, the same lines as they are written in the .md file, so any Markdown app and Claude read them too. The live card shows notes the same way.

AA row of its own, at its time

Like a screenshot row: its own time, labelled Note with a pen, a faint fill so it stands apart from what was said.

BUnder the line it was written during

A margin note inside the speaker's row, behind a thin rule, with its own time. The transcript keeps its rhythm.

CAll together above the transcript

Your notes first, as Granola does, each with its time. Click a time and the transcript goes to that moment. Click the 01:18 to try it.

03Changing a note afterwards

AClick it and type, like a speaker name

Return saves, Esc leaves it as it was, an empty note goes away. The .md changes with it.
