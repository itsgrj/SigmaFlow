Dictations no longer vanish when your AirPods connect.

## What's new

- **Connecting AirPods mid-dictation no longer loses it.** If headphones connected or disconnected while you held the key, SigmaFlow stopped hearing you and the whole dictation came out empty. Now SigmaFlow picks the microphone straight back up and keeps going. Meetings and live notes do the same.
- **If the microphone stops, SigmaFlow tells you.** When no sound comes through for a couple of seconds, SigmaFlow types and saves what it heard so far and says the microphone stopped, so you know to say the rest again.
- **No more empty rows in Dictations.** A dictation with no words in it isn't saved. If you held the key for a while, SigmaFlow says it didn't hear any words.
- **Nowhere to type? It's copied.** If the cursor isn't in a text box when you let go, SigmaFlow copies your words and says so. Paste them with ⌘V, or find them in Dictations.
- **Messages stay long enough to read.** The overlay's messages used to disappear almost at once. Now they stay for a few seconds.

## Install

Already on 1.1.0 or later? You don't need to do anything. SigmaFlow downloads this version by itself and puts it in place the next time you quit.

On 1.0 or 1.0.1, or new to SigmaFlow:

1. Download `SigmaFlow.dmg` below, open it, and drag SigmaFlow into Applications. Your recordings, dictionary and settings stay as they are.
2. Open SigmaFlow. macOS stops it the first time, because SigmaFlow isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. It never asks again, and updates don't ask either.
3. New to SigmaFlow? It walks you through two quick permissions, then you hold Right ⌘ and talk.

## You need

- macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- Room for the speech model, which SigmaFlow downloads the first time you open it
