SigmaFlow now fixes your spelling as you type in your browser, Gmail included.

## What's new

- **Spelling fixed as you type.** With the SigmaFlow extension in your browser, a misspelled word is fixed the moment you finish it, using your Mac's own spell checker and your SigmaFlow dictionary. A dotted line shows under the fix for two seconds. Works in Gmail, Cliq on the web, help desks and most text boxes. Code editors, Google Docs, Figma, addresses and emails are left alone.
- **Press Delete to keep your word.** Delete right after a fix puts your word back, and SigmaFlow remembers it: it goes into your Dictionary, marked LEARNED, so it is never fixed again.
- **Your choice.** Turn it off in Settings, Connections, or from SigmaFlow's button in your browser's toolbar.
- **A smarter Dictionary.** When you fix one real word that SigmaFlow heard as another, say "bills" to "builds", SigmaFlow no longer saves it as a rule, so every "bills" you really mean stays as you said it. Names you fix are still learned.

## Install

Already on 1.1.0 or later? You don't need to do anything. SigmaFlow downloads this version by itself and puts it in place the next time you quit.

Added the browser extension with 1.2.0? Once SigmaFlow has updated, open your browser's extensions page and click the reload arrow on SigmaFlow, just this once. From then on the extension updates itself with the app.

On 1.0 or 1.0.1, or new to SigmaFlow:

1. Download `SigmaFlow.dmg` below, open it, and drag SigmaFlow into Applications. Your recordings, dictionary and settings stay as they are.
2. Open SigmaFlow. macOS stops it the first time, because SigmaFlow isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. It never asks again, and updates don't ask either.
3. New to SigmaFlow? It walks you through two quick permissions, then you hold Right ⌘ and talk.

The browser extension isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is, Add in Settings, Connections puts it in a folder and opens your browser's extensions page: switch on Developer mode and drag the folder in.

## You need

- macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- Room for the speech model, which SigmaFlow downloads the first time you open it
- For spelling fixes: the SigmaFlow extension in Chrome, Dia, Arc, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi
