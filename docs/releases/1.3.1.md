SigmaFlow now says things more plainly, all over the app.

## What's new

- **Clearer words everywhere.** More than 60 lines across the app were rewritten so they say what you get, in plain words. Error messages say what happened and what to do next, with no codes. "Claude couldn’t answer just now, so your text wasn’t changed. Try again in a minute."
- **Transcriptions is now Dictations.** Same page, same history, the name your SigmaFlow folder already uses.
- **Insights reads at a glance.** Today shows what SigmaFlow wrote and what you typed side by side, with no sum to check. The year's heading tells you how much time you've been saving a day.
- **Transcribe meeting.** The button beside Record meeting was called Upload meeting. Your file never leaves your Mac, so the button now says what it does.
- **The menu bar matches Settings.** Clean up what you say offers the same three choices in both places: Off, Basic fixes, With Apple Intelligence.
- **Models say how your words arrive.** Each model now reads "Words appear while you talk" or "Words appear after you talk".

## Install

Already on 1.1.0 or later? You don't need to do anything. SigmaFlow downloads this version by itself and puts it in place the next time you quit.

On 1.0 or 1.0.1, or new to SigmaFlow:

1. Download `SigmaFlow.dmg` below, open it, and drag SigmaFlow into Applications. Your recordings, dictionary and settings stay as they are.
2. Open SigmaFlow. macOS stops it the first time, because SigmaFlow isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. It never asks again, and updates don't ask either.
3. New to SigmaFlow? It walks you through two quick permissions, then you hold Right ⌘ and talk.

## You need

- macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- Room for the speech model, which SigmaFlow downloads the first time you open it
