SigmaFlow now works with the AI model on your own Mac, and understands your browser.

## What's new

- **Your own model.** Use Ollama, LM Studio or any local server as the AI for your custom styles and Autowrite. No key, no account, and your text never leaves your Mac. Pick it on the Styles page: SigmaFlow finds the models you have and lists them, and opens Ollama or LM Studio for you if it isn't running.
- **SigmaFlow in your browser.** Add the SigmaFlow extension to Chrome, Dia, Arc, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi from Settings, Connections. In Gmail, Cliq on the web or a help desk, SigmaFlow now learns from the words you fix after it types, just as it does in Mail and Notes, and your saved dictation matches what you actually sent.
- **Autowrite reads the whole page.** With the extension added, Autowrite reads the entire conversation on the page, not only the part on your screen, so a reply fits a long thread.

## Install

Already on 1.1.0? You don't need to do anything. SigmaFlow downloads this version by itself and puts it in place the next time you quit.

On 1.0 or 1.0.1, or new to SigmaFlow:

1. Download `SigmaFlow.dmg` below, open it, and drag SigmaFlow into Applications. Your recordings, dictionary and settings stay as they are.
2. Open SigmaFlow. macOS stops it the first time, because SigmaFlow isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. It never asks again, and updates don't ask either.
3. New to SigmaFlow? It walks you through two quick permissions, then you hold Right ⌘ and talk.

The browser extension isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is, Add puts it in a folder and opens your browser's extensions page: switch on Developer mode and drag the folder in.

## You need

- macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- Room for the speech model, which SigmaFlow downloads the first time you open it
- For your own model: Ollama or LM Studio with a model downloaded
