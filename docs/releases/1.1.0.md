SigmaFlow now keeps itself up to date. This is the last version you download by hand.

## What's new

- **Updates by itself.** Once a day SigmaFlow looks for a new version, downloads it quietly, and puts it in place the next time you quit. It never interrupts a dictation, a meeting or your notes. When one is waiting, the version at the foot of the sidebar says so, with Restart if you want it now.
- **Meetings can be filmed.** When a call starts, choose With video and SigmaFlow records the call's window too. Open the transcript and the picture plays beside the lines, on one clock, with stills of whatever was on screen.
- **A call says where it was.** A meeting recorded during a call is labelled with the app or the site it was on, Zoom, Google Meet or Cliq, so a call and a room meeting no longer look the same.
- **No more ringing in the transcript.** Ringtones and hang-up beeps are left out before SigmaFlow listens, so they no longer turn into rows of nonsense.
- **Your words, fixed as you talk.** The overlay shows your dictionary's fixes while you speak, not only after you let go.

## Install

1. Download `SigmaFlow.dmg` below, open it, and drag SigmaFlow into Applications. If you have 1.0 or 1.0.1, replace it. Your recordings, dictionary and settings stay as they are.
2. Open SigmaFlow. macOS stops it the first time, because SigmaFlow isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. It never asks again, and updates don't ask either.
3. New to SigmaFlow? It walks you through two quick permissions, then you hold Right ⌘ and talk.

## You need

- macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- Room for the speech model, which SigmaFlow downloads the first time you open it
