# Code of conduct

SigmaFlow is made by people for people. Everyone who opens an issue, comments or sends a pull
request is welcome here, whoever they are and however much they know.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Talk about the work, never the person.
- Take feedback as help, and give it the same way.
- Respect a no. SigmaFlow turns down features often; it is never personal.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private details without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find out of place in a friendly workplace.

## If something happens

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or profile. GitHub's team reviews
every report.

The maintainer may hide, edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block
anyone who doesn't follow this code, here or anywhere else the project lives.
